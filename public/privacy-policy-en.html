Privacy Policy

App: Coffee Finder (近くの自家焙煎豆を探す)
Effective and last updated: November 12, 2026

Coffee Finder helps you discover coffee beans from home-roasting shops near you. The operator (the "Operator") takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is stored, and which services it communicates with.

In short
  • No account is required. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or similar details.
  • Your collection, ratings, tasting records, package photos, and similar data are stored only on your device. The Operator does not collect them.
  • Your location is used only to sort results by distance. It is never sent to or stored on the Operator's servers.
  • The app currently uses no advertising and no analytics tools.

1. Operator

OperatorSAI
ContactDirect message to the X (formerly Twitter) account @SaiCoffea

2. Information the Operator does not collect

The app has no sign-up or login, and the Operator runs no server that receives or stores your information. Accordingly, the Operator does not collect, store, or use any of the following:

  • Information that identifies you, such as your name, email address, phone number, or address
  • Your location
  • The records and photos you save in the app

3. Information stored on your device

To provide its features, the app stores the following information on your device (in the browser storage used by the app). This information is not sent off your device, and the Operator cannot see it.

  • Your collection (saved products and shops, and the dates you saved them)
  • Shops and products you register manually (product name, price, weight, origin, roast level, processing method, flavors, notes, and registration date)
  • Your star ratings for products
  • My tools (the name, maker, JAN/GTIN, product page URL, notes and registration date of the brewing tools you register)
  • A record of the name, shop and origin of products you saved, rated or logged (so the name is still known if the product is later removed from the product data)
  • Tasting logs and shop tasting notes (brewing conditions, impressions, flavors, and registration date and time)
  • Package photos (see Section 4)
  • Your viewing history, and the previous state used to notify you of new arrivals and stock changes
  • Achievements you have earned (with the dates), and the dates you added package photos
  • Your settings (display language, color theme, registered area, display range, and the trial state of premium features)

This information is lost if you clear the browser's site data, clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or change devices, and the Operator cannot restore it. If needed, use "Data backup" in the app's Settings to export your data to a file and keep it safe. The file contains your records, such as your collection, star ratings, tasting logs, tasting notes and My tools. The file is saved on your device and is not sent to the Operator. Please note that if you give the file to someone else, your notes and impressions go with it.

4. Package photos

  • Package photos are limited to the photos you take or choose from your gallery. Photos are stored only on your device. The Operator does not send, collect, or publish them to any server or elsewhere.
  • The app reads only the individual photo you choose. It does not read any other photos on your device.
  • When saving, the app shrinks the photo and saves it as a new image. As a result, metadata in the original photo, such as the location where it was taken (Exif), is not carried over to the saved photo.
  • When you delete a product, its photo is deleted with it. You can also delete just the photo.
  • Photos are included in a backup file only if you select "Include package photos" in "Data backup." The file is saved on your device and is not sent to the Operator. Please note that if you give the file to someone else, the photos go with it.

5. Location

  • Your location is used only to sort nearby shops and products by distance.
  • Using your location requires your permission on your device. If you do not allow it, you can still use the app with other sort orders, such as newest first.
  • Your location is used only temporarily, on your device, to calculate distances. It is not sent to the Operator's servers, and it is not saved on your device.
  • You can withdraw the permission at any time in your device or browser settings.

6. Communication with external services

The app communicates with the following external services as needed to display its content. By the nature of internet communication, these services receive information such as your device's IP address and browser type. The Operator does not receive this information. Each service handles it under its own privacy policy.

ServicePurpose
GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com)Retrieving shop and product data
OpenStreetMap (tile servers)Displaying maps (map images for the visible area are retrieved when you open a map)
Google FontsDisplaying web fonts

Links within the app (such as "Open in map" to Google Maps, shops' official websites and Instagram pages, and the Operator's X account) take you to those services or external sites. Their handling of your information is governed by their own policies.

7. Disclosure to third parties

Because the Operator does not collect your information, it does not provide any of it to third parties.

8. Information about listed shops and products

Information about the shops and products listed in the app is based on publicly available sources, such as each shop's official website. If you represent a listed shop and would like a correction or removal, please contact us using the contact details in Section 1.

9. Advertising, analytics, and payments

At present, the app provides no advertising, analytics, or payment features (the "Premium" option in Settings is a trial display for development). Before introducing any of these, we will update this policy with the external services used, the information collected, and the purposes.

10. Managing and deleting your information

  • Information on your device is managed on your own device. Please take care against loss or theft of your device.
  • You can delete all information on your device by uninstalling the app or clearing the browser's or the app's data.
  • Because the Operator does not store your information, there is no information on the Operator's side to request deletion of.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in law or the addition of features. For significant changes, we will notify you in the app or on this page. The revised policy takes effect when it is posted on this page.

12. Contact

For questions about this policy or how information is handled, please contact us using the contact details in Section 1.
